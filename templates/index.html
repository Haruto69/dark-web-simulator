Deterministic counterfactual cybersecurity training

Make a real decision inside a realistic scenario, see the consequence play out, then rewind and compare it against the path you didn't take. No terminology quiz, no lecture slides -- just the decision and what happens next.

Start training Instructor access

How it works

  1. Observe a realistic scenario -- an email, a prompt, a workstation.
  2. Decide what you would actually do, in the moment.
  3. Experience the consequence your decision leads to.
  4. Rewind to the same decision point.
  5. Compare your path against the one you didn't take.
  6. Reflect on what changed your judgment.
