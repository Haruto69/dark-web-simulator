🌐 Essential Dark Web & Cybersecurity Safety

10 Commandments of Digital Safety

  1. Never Enter Real Credentials - Don't use real passwords on suspicious sites, even for testing
  2. Use Strong, Unique Passwords - Every account should have a different password managed by a password manager
  3. Enable Two-Factor Authentication - Add an extra layer of security to all critical accounts
  4. Keep Software Updated - Install security patches promptly to close vulnerabilities
  5. Back Up Your Data Regularly - Follow the 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies, 2 different media, 1 offsite
  6. Be Skeptical of Urgency - Scammers create false urgency to bypass your critical thinking
  7. Verify Before You Click - Hover over links, check URLs, and confirm sender identities
  8. Use Reputable Security Software - Install and maintain antivirus, anti-malware, and firewall protection
  9. Educate Yourself Continuously - Cyber threats evolve; stay informed about new tactics
  10. Report Suspicious Activity - Alert your IT team or authorities about potential threats

⚡ Quick Reference

🚨 Red Flags

  • Urgent language
  • Misspelled URLs
  • Requests for credentials
  • Too-good-to-be-true offers
  • Generic greetings
  • Suspicious attachments

✅ Green Flags

  • HTTPS & padlock icon
  • Exact domain match
  • Personalized content
  • No credential requests
  • Professional formatting
  • Verifiable sender

⚠️ When In Doubt

  • Don't click or download
  • Contact sender directly
  • Report to IT/security
  • Use official website URL
  • Verify phone numbers
  • Trust your instincts
