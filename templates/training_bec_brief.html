Business Email Compromise

Before you start, four things about how this works.

  • You are handling accounts payable for {{ org_name }}, an invented organisation, and {{ supplier }} is an invented supplier.
  • The exercise begins with one outstanding invoice and a request to change where it is paid.
  • Your response is carried out against a synthetic payment workflow, and you will see exactly what it produced.
  • Afterwards the request is returned to the identical starting state, a different response is carried out, and the two are compared.

There is no payment system behind this exercise. The invoice, the amount and any loss are authored scenario values held in memory; RewindSec does not initiate or simulate a networked financial transaction, and you are never asked for a real bank detail.
