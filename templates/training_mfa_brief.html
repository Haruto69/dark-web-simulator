MFA Fatigue

Before you start, four things about how this works.

  • You are looking at a synthetic account on an invented identity service, {{ identity_service }}. Nothing here belongs to a real provider.
  • The exercise begins the moment an approval request you were not expecting arrives.
  • Your response is carried out against a synthetic account state, and you will see exactly what it produced.
  • Afterwards the account is returned to the identical starting state, a different response is carried out, and the two are compared.

No identity provider is contacted, no real approval is issued and no account of yours is involved. The approval and the access that follows it are local synthetic state changes.
