Ransomware Incident Response

Before you start, four things about how this works.

  • You are looking at a synthetic workstation holding {{ files_total }} invented documents. Nothing on it is real, and nothing outside the sandbox is touched.
  • The exercise begins at the moment something has already gone wrong: one document is no longer available.
  • Your response is carried out for real inside a disposable container, and you will see the state it produced.
  • Afterwards the workstation is returned to exactly the same starting point, a different response is carried out, and the two are compared.
